2. Day 2 · The 3-point structure

4. Day 4 · Eye contact without staring

Your heart racing before you speak isn't a sign you're bad at it. It's your body getting ready.

Try this before your next talk: breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out for 6. Twice. Your voice will steady.
