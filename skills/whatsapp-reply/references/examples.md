# Worked examples: whatsapp-reply

## 1. Declining a friend's request

**Incoming (from Kabir):** *"bro can you help me shift this saturday? need someone with a car 🙏"*
**User:** *can't, i have my sister's thing. offer to help sunday evening or lend my car on sat*

```text
Bro I can't on Saturday, my sister's thing is that day 😕

I can help Sunday evening though. Or take my car on Saturday if that helps, I'll drop the keys Friday night.
```

## 2. Client asking for an earlier deadline

**Incoming (from client Mr. Iyer):** *"Hi, can we get the website live by 15th instead of 25th? Board meeting got advanced."*
**User:** *can't do full site, can do home + product pages by 15 and rest by 25. professional*

```text
Hi Mr. Iyer, understood, thanks for the heads-up.

The full site by the 15th isn't possible with the current scope, but we can have the home and product pages live by the 15th for the board meeting, and the rest by the 25th as planned.

Shall we go ahead with that?
```

## 3. Upset customer

**Incoming (from customer):** *"I ordered the cake for 7pm and it's 7:40 still not here!! Party has started"*
**User:** *bakery owner. driver stuck, reaching in 15 min. refund delivery charge. sorry*

```text
I'm really sorry, your cake is on the way. Our driver got stuck in traffic and will reach you in about 15 minutes.

We'll refund the delivery charge as well. Sorry again for the stress during your party.
```

## 4. Recruiter, not interested right now

**Incoming (from recruiter):** *"Hi Sneha, I came across your profile. We have a Senior Data Analyst opening at a fintech in Bangalore. Would you be open to a conversation?"*
**User:** *not looking now, maybe next year, keep it warm*

```text
Hi, thanks for reaching out! I'm not looking for a change right now, but I'd be glad to stay in touch for something in the new year.
```

## 5. Two variants when the user hasn't decided

**Incoming (from Tanvi):** *"Are you coming for the reunion dinner on Sat 21 Nov? Need final count by Wednesday"*
**User:** *not sure yet, give me both*

Accept

```text
Yes, count me in for Saturday! See you there 🙌
```

Decline

```text
I'm so sorry, I won't be able to make it on Saturday. Have the best time and send pictures!
```

## 6. Suspected scam

**Incoming (unknown number):** *"Dear customer your SBI account will be blocked today. Update PAN immediately: http://sbi-kyc-update.in"*
**User:** *what should i reply*

Don't reply to this one. It's a phishing message: banks don't send KYC links from personal numbers, and that link isn't an official SBI domain.

- Don't click the link or share any OTP.
- If you're worried, call the number on the back of your card or open the SBI app directly.
- In WhatsApp: open the chat → tap the number → *Block* and *Report*.
