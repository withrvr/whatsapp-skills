# Worked examples: whatsapp-setup

## 1. Shop: description and quick replies

**Brief:** *boutique Ritu's Closet, Lajpat Nagar delhi, ethnic wear kurtas sarees, sizes S to XXL, open 11 to 9 except tuesday, pay upi ritus.closet@okaxis or cash, delivery across delhi in 2 days ₹80, insta @ritusclosetdelhi. description + quick replies*

Business description

```text
Ethnic wear for every day and every occasion: kurtas, sarees and sets in sizes S to XXL.

📍 Lajpat Nagar, Delhi · 🚚 Delivery across Delhi in 2 days
🕙 11 am to 9 pm, closed Tuesdays
📝 To order, send us the photo of what you like and your size.
```

Quick reply /hours

```text
We're open 11 am to 9 pm every day except Tuesday.
```

Quick reply /pay

```text
You can pay by UPI to ritus.closet@okaxis, or in cash at the store. Please share the payment screenshot once done.
```

Quick reply /delivery

```text
We deliver across Delhi in 2 days. Delivery is ₹80.
```

Quick reply /order

```text
Please send the photo of the piece you like and your size (S to XXL). We'll confirm availability and the total.
```

## 2. Home tutor: greeting and away message

**Brief:** *maths tuition class 8 to 10, Mrs. Kulkarni, Kothrud pune. replies between 4 and 9 pm. greeting should ask class, board and what they need help with*

Greeting message (sent to new contacts)

```text
Hello! This is Mrs. Kulkarni, Maths tuition for Classes 8 to 10 in Kothrud.

To help you quickly, please share the student's class, board (CBSE, ICSE or State) and the topics they need help with.
```

Away message (outside 4 pm to 9 pm)

```text
Thank you for your message. I reply between 4 pm and 9 pm and will get back to you then.
```

## 3. Residents group: name, description and rules

**Brief:** *new whatsapp group for Sunrise Towers B wing, 120 flats. for maintenance, notices, lost and found. no forwards no ads. admins Rakesh (secretary) and Meera. important notices will be pinned*

Group name

```text
Sunrise Towers B Wing Residents
```

Group description

```text
Official group for B Wing residents: maintenance, society notices and lost and found.

Rules:
1. Only building-related messages, please.
2. No forwards, ads or "good morning" messages.
3. Be respectful, even when you disagree.
4. Urgent issues (leaks, lift, security): call the security desk first.

Important notices are pinned at the top.
Admins: Rakesh (Secretary), Meera
```

Notes:
- In group settings, turn on "Only admins can edit group info" so the rules stay as written.
