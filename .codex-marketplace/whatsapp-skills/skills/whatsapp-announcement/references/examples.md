```text
📢 *Class 7B: PTM on Sat, 14 Nov*

Dear Parents, the Parent-Teacher Meeting is on *Sat, 14 Nov, 9 am to 12 noon*. Please come in your child's slot.

🕘 *Slots:* https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1aB2cD
✅ Please bring the signed report card.

📝 *Also:* Maths unit test on *Wed, 18 Nov*, chapters 4 to 6.

Mrs. Fernandes, Class Teacher
```
