# Invitation types and the invite sequence

## The sequence

| Message | When to send | Length | What it carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| Save the date | 6-12 weeks before (weddings, destination events) | short | Names, occasion, date(s), city. "Invite to follow." |
| Invite | 2-4 weeks before (1-2 weeks for parties) | medium | Everything: functions, times, venues, map links, RSVP |
| E-card caption | With the card / video | short-medium | Opener + key details in text + RSVP |
| Personal invite to elders | With or after a phone call | short | Respectful, personal, from the host by name |
| Reminder | 1-2 days before | short | Function, time, venue link, contact |
| Day-of | Morning of / 2-3 hours before | one-liner-short | Map link, parking, contact on the spot |
| Thank-you | Within 1-2 days after | short | Gratitude, photos link if any |

The user usually asks for one. Offer the next one in Notes only if it's useful ("Want the reminder for Friday too?").

## Openers by occasion

- **Wedding (family voice):** "With great joy, the Sharma family invites you to the wedding of *Ananya* and *Rohit*."
- **Wedding (couple voice):** "We're getting married! 💍 *Ananya & Rohit* would love you to be there."
- **Engagement / roka / sagai:** "*Ananya & Rohit* are getting engaged and we'd love to celebrate with you."
- **Birthday (kid):** "🎈 *Aarav is turning 5!* Join us for cake, games and lots of fun."
- **Birthday (adult, milestone):** "Join us to celebrate *Papa's 60th birthday*."
- **Housewarming / griha pravesh:** "🏡 We've moved into our new home and would love to celebrate with you."
- **Baby shower / godh bharai:** "Join us for *Pooja's baby shower* 💛"
- **Naming ceremony / naamkaran:** "With joy, we invite you to the naming ceremony of our little one."
- **Pooja / religious event:** "By God's grace, we are holding a *Satyanarayan Pooja* at our home and request your presence."
- **Anniversary:** "*25 years together!* Join us to celebrate Mummy and Papa's silver anniversary."
- **Farewell (office):** "Join us to say goodbye to *Neha* before she moves to the London office."
- **Reunion:** "*Batch of 2016, it's been 10 years!* Reunion dinner, be there."
- **Launch / opening:** "We're opening our new store and would love you to be among our first guests."
- **Meetup / community event:** "Next meetup: *[topic]*, [date]. Open to all members."

## Details block

One function = one block. Order by date. Keep labels consistent across blocks.

```text
*Mehendi*
📅 Fri, 11 Dec · 4 pm onwards
📍 Sharma Residence, 12 Civil Lines, Jaipur
https://maps.app.goo.gl/AbCd

*Wedding*
📅 Sat, 12 Dec · Baraat 7 pm, Pheras 9 pm
📍 The Leela Palace, Jaipur
https://maps.app.goo.gl/EfGh
```

Optional lines, only when the user gives them:
`👗 Dress code:` · `🅿️ Parking:` · `👶 Kids:` · `🍽️ Dinner:` · `🎁` (gift note in the user's words) · `🏨 Stay:`

## RSVP line

- "Please let us know by *Sun, 29 Nov* if you can make it: reply here or call Rohan, +91 98111 22334."
- For headcount on choices (veg/non-veg, attending which functions), suggest a **poll** or a form in Notes.
- For big family weddings: often no RSVP line. Follow the user.

## Close

- Family: "Your presence and blessings will make the day complete. 🙏" / "With love, the Sharma family"
- Friends: "Can't wait to see you there!"
- Office: "Hope to see you all there!" + organiser name

## Personal invite to elders (after or instead of a call)

Short, from the host by name, respectful:

```text
Namaste Mama ji 🙏

Ananya's wedding is on Sat, 12 Dec in Jaipur. Papa and I would be so happy to have you and Mami ji with us for all the functions.

I've shared the card in the family group. I'll call you this weekend for your travel plans.

– Pooja
```


## Thank-you

```text
Thank you all so much for being part of Ananya and Rohit's wedding ❤️

Your love and blessings made every function special. Photos are here: [link]

– The Sharma family
```

## Language patterns

- **Hindi (Devanagari)**: "सादर आमंत्रण", "आपकी उपस्थिति हमारे लिए आशीर्वाद होगी", dates as "शनिवार, 12 दिसंबर".
- **Hinglish**: "Aap sab zaroor aana!", "Milte hain!", "Aapka intezaar rahega".
- **Bilingual**: full invite in the first language, a blank line, the full invite in the second. Names and links appear in both.
- Regional languages: write natively, keep names and links identical, ask the user for spellings of names in that script when unsure.
