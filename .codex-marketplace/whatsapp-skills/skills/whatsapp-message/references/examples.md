# Worked examples: whatsapp-message

Each brief is written the way people actually type it: lowercase, out of order,
half in their head. The output is exactly what the skill delivers.

## 1. Late to a client call

**Brief:** *stuck in traffic, client call with Mr. Mehta at 11, will be 10 min late, can join audio from car if he wants, keep it professional*

```text
Good morning Mr. Mehta, I'm stuck in traffic and will be about 10 minutes late for our 11 am call.

If you'd prefer to start on time, I can join on audio from the car. Whatever works for you.
```

## 2. First message to a referral

**Brief:** *got number from Ankit Rao (we were in same batch at IIM). she's head of design at Finlo. i want 15 min to ask about their design hiring, i'm a product designer 6 yrs. not pushy*

```text
Hi Meera, I'm Rohan, a product designer. Ankit Rao (we were batchmates at IIM) shared your number and said you might not mind a quick question.

I've been designing fintech products for 6 years and I'm curious how the design team at Finlo is growing. Would you have 15 minutes for a call sometime next week?

Totally fine if not, thanks either way!
```

## 4. Leave request with dates

**Brief:** *to manager Sana. need leave thu 12 nov to fri 13 nov, cousin's wedding in jaipur. Neha covering the vendor calls. will check mail once a day*

```text
Hi Sana, I'd like to take leave on Thu, 12 Nov and Fri, 13 Nov for my cousin's wedding in Jaipur.

Neha will cover the vendor calls, and I'll check email once a day in case anything urgent comes up.

I'll apply on the HR portal as well. Is that okay?
```

## 5. Complaint to society office

**Brief:** *flat B-1204, water leaking from ceiling in bathroom since sunday, upstairs flat B-1304 locked, need plumber to check by tomorrow, i've sent photos. firm but polite*

```text
Hello, this is Priya from B-1204.

Water has been leaking from our bathroom ceiling since Sunday. It seems to be coming from B-1304, which is locked.

Could you please arrange for the plumber to check by tomorrow and contact the B-1304 owner? I've sent photos of the leak above.

Thank you.
```

## 6. Condolence to a colleague

**Brief:** *colleague Arjun lost his father, i met him once at the office day, he was very kind. want to say sorry and he doesn't need to worry about the release*

```text
Arjun, I'm so sorry about your father. I remember meeting him at the office day, he was so kind to everyone.

Please don't worry about the release at all, we've got it covered. Take all the time you need, no need to reply.
```

## 7. Asking the boss for a meeting about a raise

**Brief:** *ask my manager Dev for 20 min this week to discuss my compensation, my appraisal was done in sep and i want to talk about the revision. short*

```text
Hi Dev, could we find 20 minutes this week to talk about my compensation revision after the September appraisal? Happy to work around your calendar.
```

## 8. Congratulating a friend, funny

**Brief:** *my friend Isha got into the Berlin masters she's been trying 2 yrs, funny, she always complained about german grammar*

```text
ISHA BERLIN?! 🎉 Two years of fighting German grammar and German grammar finally gave up.

So proud of you. Celebration is on you, obviously, but I'll bring the cake.
```
